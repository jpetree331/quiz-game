# Arts & words
Find a little inspiration in color, music, and the words we use.

## What is a painting of arranged objects such as fruit or flowers called?
- [x] Still life
- [ ] Self-portrait
- [ ] Landscape
- [ ] Seascape
> A still life makes inanimate objects its main subject.

## What do we call a painting an artist makes of themself?
- [x] Self-portrait
- [ ] Mural
- [ ] Mosaic
- [ ] Still life
> The artist is also the subject of a self-portrait.

## Which art form assembles small pieces of tile, stone, or glass into an image?
- [x] Mosaic
- [ ] Etching
- [ ] Origami
- [ ] Calligraphy
> A mosaic builds a larger image from many small pieces.

## What is the Japanese art of folding paper called?
- [x] Origami
- [ ] Pottery
- [ ] Weaving
- [ ] Embroidery
> Paper folds create shapes without needing a painted image.

## Which tool is most closely associated with a sculptor carving stone?
- [x] Chisel
- [ ] Violin bow
- [ ] Loom
- [ ] Camera lens
> A chisel removes pieces of material to shape a sculpture.

## In traditional paint mixing, blue and yellow usually make which color?
- [x] Green
- [ ] Purple
- [ ] Orange
- [ ] Pink
> This is the familiar pigment-mixing model taught in painting.

## Which word describes how light or dark a color appears in visual art?
- [x] Value
- [ ] Tempo
- [ ] Rhyme
- [ ] Plot
> Value helps an artist create contrast and the illusion of form.

## What is a large painting made directly on a wall called?
- [x] Mural
- [ ] Sonnet
- [ ] Sketchbook
- [ ] Print run
> A mural is part of a wall or other architectural surface.

## What is calligraphy the art of?
- [x] Decorative writing
- [ ] Stone carving
- [ ] Paper folding
- [ ] Glass blowing
> Calligraphy pays special attention to the form of written marks.

## Which of these is a woodwind instrument?
- [x] Clarinet
- [ ] Trumpet
- [ ] Trombone
- [ ] Tuba
> The clarinet uses a reed to help produce its sound.

## How many strings does a standard violin have?
- [x] Four
- [ ] Three
- [ ] Five
- [ ] Six
> The usual tuning, from low to high, is G, D, A, E.

## What does tempo describe in music?
- [x] Speed
- [ ] Volume
- [ ] Pitch range
- [ ] Instrument size
> Tempo tells us how fast or slowly music moves.

## What does the musical direction "piano" mean?
- [x] Softly
- [ ] Loudly
- [ ] Quickly
- [ ] Twice
> Piano is a dynamic marking as well as the name of an instrument.

## How many lines are on a standard musical staff?
- [x] Five
- [ ] Four
- [ ] Six
- [ ] Seven
> Notes can sit on the lines or in the spaces between them.

## Which instrument has black and white keys and strings struck by hammers?
- [x] Piano
- [ ] Flute
- [ ] Cello
- [ ] Cymbal
> Pressing a piano key sets a hammer mechanism in motion.

## What is a group of people singing together called?
- [x] Choir
- [ ] Gallery
- [ ] Cast list
- [ ] Canvas
> A choir can sing with or without instrumental accompaniment.

## Which punctuation mark usually ends a direct question in English?
- [x] Question mark
- [ ] Comma
- [ ] Colon
- [ ] Apostrophe
> A question mark signals a direct question to the reader.

## Which pair of words are synonyms?
- [x] Happy and joyful
- [ ] Hot and cold
- [ ] Early and late
- [ ] Full and empty
> Synonyms have similar meanings.

## Which pair of words are antonyms?
- [x] Ancient and modern
- [ ] Small and tiny
- [ ] Fast and quick
- [ ] Calm and peaceful
> Antonyms have opposite meanings.

## What is the plural of "child"?
- [x] Children
- [ ] Childs
- [ ] Childes
- [ ] Childrens
> Children is an irregular plural.

## Which word is a verb in "Birds sing at dawn"?
- [x] Sing
- [ ] Birds
- [ ] At
- [ ] Dawn
> The verb tells us what the birds do.

## Which word is an adjective in "The quiet street"?
- [x] Quiet
- [ ] The
- [ ] Street
- [ ] None of them
> An adjective can describe a noun.

## What is a comparison using "like" or "as" called?
- [x] Simile
- [ ] Anagram
- [ ] Palindrome
- [ ] Footnote
> A simile makes an explicit comparison.

## What is a word that reads the same backward and forward called?
- [x] Palindrome
- [ ] Synonym
- [ ] Antonym
- [ ] Acronym
> Level is an example of a palindrome.

## What do you make by rearranging a word's letters into another word?
- [x] Anagram
- [ ] Paragraph
- [ ] Haiku
- [ ] Caption
> For example, listen can be rearranged into silent.

## How many lines are in a traditional sonnet?
- [x] Fourteen
- [ ] Ten
- [ ] Twelve
- [ ] Sixteen
> A sonnet traditionally contains fourteen lines.

## In a story, who is the narrator?
- [x] The voice telling the story
- [ ] Always the author as a character
- [ ] Always the villain
- [ ] The person printing the book
> The narrator is the voice or perspective through which a story is told.

## What is the setting of a story?
- [x] Its time and place
- [ ] Its total word count
- [ ] Its cover design
- [ ] Its list price
> Setting helps establish where and when the events happen.

## What is the term for the main character in a story?
- [x] Protagonist
- [ ] Editor
- [ ] Publisher
- [ ] Illustrator
> The protagonist is the central character in the narrative.

## What is a sculpture designed to be viewed from all sides called?
- [x] Sculpture in the round
- [ ] A footnote
- [ ] A fresco
- [ ] A woodcut
> Sculpture in the round occupies three-dimensional space rather than lying against a flat background.
