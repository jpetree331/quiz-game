# Science basics
Everyday science from the periodic table to the solar system.

## What is the chemical symbol for gold?
- [x] Au
- [ ] Ag
- [ ] Go
- [ ] Gd
> Au comes from the Latin word aurum. Ag is silver.

## At sea level, water boils at what temperature?
- [x] 100 °C
- [ ] 90 °C
- [ ] 110 °C
- [ ] 120 °C
> Higher up a mountain the air pressure is lower, so water boils below 100 °C.

## Which gas do plants take in to make their food?
- [x] Carbon dioxide
- [ ] Oxygen
- [ ] Nitrogen
- [ ] Hydrogen
> Plants use carbon dioxide, water and sunlight, and give off oxygen.

## What is at the center of an atom?
- [x] The nucleus
- [ ] An electron
- [ ] A molecule
- [ ] A photon
> The nucleus holds the protons and neutrons; electrons move around it.

## Which planet is closest to the Sun?
- [x] Mercury
- [ ] Venus
- [ ] Earth
- [ ] Mars

## What is the pH of pure water at room temperature?
- [x] 7
- [ ] 0
- [ ] 5
- [ ] 14
> 7 is neutral. Lower is acidic, higher is basic.

## Which part of the cell is often called its powerhouse?
- [x] Mitochondria
- [ ] Nucleus
- [ ] Ribosome
- [ ] Cell wall

## What is the hardest naturally occurring material?
- [x] Diamond
- [ ] Steel
- [ ] Quartz
- [ ] Granite
> Diamond is pure carbon arranged in a very rigid crystal.

## Which gas makes up most of the air we breathe?
- [x] Nitrogen
- [ ] Oxygen
- [ ] Carbon dioxide
- [ ] Argon
> About 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen.

## Roughly how fast does light travel in empty space?
- [x] 300,000 km per second
- [ ] 300 km per second
- [ ] 30,000 km per second
- [ ] 3,000,000 km per second

## Which unit measures electric current?
- [x] Ampere
- [ ] Volt
- [ ] Watt
- [ ] Ohm
> Volts measure voltage, watts measure power, ohms measure resistance.

## Which element has atomic number 1?
- [x] Hydrogen
- [ ] Helium
- [ ] Oxygen
- [ ] Carbon
> It has one proton, which is what the atomic number counts.
