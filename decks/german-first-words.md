# German — first words
Twelve words you will hear on day one.

## What does "Danke" mean?
- [x] Thank you
- [ ] Please
- [ ] Hello
- [ ] Sorry
> Said like DAHN-kuh.

## What does "Bitte" mean?
- [x] Please (and also "you're welcome")
- [ ] Goodbye
- [ ] Yes
- [ ] Water
> One word does double duty: it is both "please" and the reply to "Danke".

## What is "der Hund"?
- [x] The dog
- [ ] The cat
- [ ] The horse
- [ ] The bird

## What is "die Katze"?
- [x] The cat
- [ ] The dog
- [ ] The mouse
- [ ] The cow

## What is "das Wasser"?
- [x] Water
- [ ] Bread
- [ ] Milk
- [ ] Beer

## What is "das Brot"?
- [x] Bread
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Cheese
- [ ] Cake

## What does "Guten Morgen" mean?
- [x] Good morning
- [ ] Good night
- [ ] Good evening
- [ ] Good luck

## What does "Tschüss" mean?
- [x] Bye
- [ ] Cheers
- [ ] Thanks
- [ ] Excuse me
> Said like CHOOSS. Casual, for friends.

## What does "Ja" mean?
- [x] Yes
- [ ] No
- [ ] Maybe
- [ ] Now

## What is "das Haus"?
- [x] The house
- [ ] The car
- [ ] The street
- [ ] The garden

## What is "der Apfel"?
- [x] The apple
- [ ] The pear
- [ ] The orange
- [ ] The plum

## What is "der Zug"?
- [x] The train
- [ ] The bus
- [ ] The plane
- [ ] The bicycle
> The one you are probably sitting on right now.
